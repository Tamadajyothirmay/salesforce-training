# Day 1 - CRM Basics

## 1. What is CRM?

CRM stands for Customer Relationship Management.  
It is a system used by companies to manage customer data, sales, communication, support, and business processes in one place.

CRM helps companies:
- Store customer information
- Track sales activities
- Improve customer relationships
- Increase business productivity
- Automate business processes

Example CRM tools:
- Salesforce
- HubSpot
- Zoho CRM
- Microsoft Dynamics

---

## 2. Why Companies Use Salesforce

Salesforce is a cloud-based CRM platform used by companies to manage customers and business operations.

Companies use Salesforce because it:
- Stores customer data securely
- Improves sales tracking
- Automates workflows
- Provides reports and dashboards
- Helps customer support teams
- Supports mobile and cloud access
- Allows customization based on company needs

Salesforce is widely used because it is scalable, secure, and easy to integrate with other applications.

---

## 3. Explain the Following

### Account
An Account represents a company or organization in Salesforce.

Example:
- TCS
- Infosys
- Amazon

Accounts store company details like:
- Company name
- Address
- Industry
- Phone number

---

### Contact
A Contact represents a person associated with an Account.

Example:
- Employee
- Customer
- Manager

Contact stores information like:
- Name
- Email
- Phone number
- Job title

Example:
- Account: Amazon
- Contact: Rajesh Kumar (Manager at Amazon)

---

### Opportunity
An Opportunity represents a potential sales deal or business deal.

It helps companies track:
- Deal value
- Sales stage
- Expected revenue
- Closing date

Example:
A company discussing software purchase with a client is considered an Opportunity.

---

## 4. Real-World Mapping
Example: College Admission System
Salesforce Object	|Real-World Mapping
Account          	|College
Contact	          |Student or Parent
Opportunity	      |Student Admission Process
Explanation
Account → Represents the college or educational institution.
Contact → Represents the student or parent associated with the college.
Opportunity → Represents the chance of a student taking admission into the college.
